Loki is a fictional character from Marvel Comics and the Marvel Cinematic Universe, known as the God of Mischief and adoptive brother of Thor. Clever, manipulative, and endlessly charming, he shifts between villain and reluctant hero depending on the story, often driven by a deep sense of insecurity and a desire to prove his worth after growing up in Thor's shadow. Over the years — especially through his own solo Disney+ series — Loki evolved from a straightforward antagonist into one of Marvel's most complex and beloved characters, exploring themes of identity, destiny, and self-acceptance.
